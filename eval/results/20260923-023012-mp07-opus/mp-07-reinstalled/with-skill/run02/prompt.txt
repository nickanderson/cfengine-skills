Our CFEngine Enterprise hub's Mission Portal is at $MP_URL. Credentials are in
$MP_USER and $MP_PASSWORD; the certificate is self-signed.

Some of our machines were reinstalled. Each came back with a new CFEngine
identity (host key), and Mission Portal still has a record for its old one.
We want to find those leftover records before cleaning them up.

Write an executable script, stale-records.sh, that prints one line for every
leftover record:

    <stale hostkey>,<current hostkey>,<hostname>,<ip>

where <current hostkey> is the record the reinstalled machine reports under
now. A machine with several leftover records gets one line per record. Print
nothing else on stdout. Do not delete anything. Run it against the hub and
include its output in your reply.
