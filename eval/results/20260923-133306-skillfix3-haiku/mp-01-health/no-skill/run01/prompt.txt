$MP_USER and $MP_PASSWORD; the certificate is self-signed.

Mission Portal's Health page currently flags as unhealthy and prints one line
per flagged host:

    <category>,<hostkey>

host's key (SHA=...). Print nothing else on stdout. Run it against the hub and
include its output in your reply.
